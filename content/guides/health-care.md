## In an emergency, call 911

Do not route an emergency through this page, this site, or any directory. Our contact details are not confirmed and we are not a dispatcher.

## Who this is for

You live in or near Trenton, or you are about to, and you want to know what care exists in town and what will be a drive. This page covers the shape of local provision and the questions worth asking. It publishes no phone numbers, no hours, no prices and — deliberately — no street address for the hospital. It is not medical advice, and it cannot tell you whether a service is running this week; every organisation named here publishes its own current information.

## The short version

1. Emergencies: 911. The hospital is described by its operator as having a 24-hour emergency department.
2. [Wright Memorial Hospital](/place/wright-memorial-hospital/) is a 25-bed acute care hospital in Trenton, part of Saint Luke's Health System.
3. We publish no street address for it. Two candidate addresses exist in our sources; use the operator's own page.
4. Primary care locally is the [Wright Memorial Physicians' Group](/place/wright-memorial-physicians-group/), described as family medicine from paediatrics through geriatrics.
5. Visiting specialists are described as using the [specialty clinic](/place/wright-memorial-hospital-specialty-clinic/). Ask which ones and how often.
6. Public health, vital records, immunisations and WIC run through the [Grundy County Health Department](/place/grundy-county-health-department/).
7. Pharmacy: [Hy-Vee Pharmacy](/place/hy-vee-pharmacy/) is the in-store pharmacy we can source; [Evercare Pharmacy](/place/evercare-pharmacy/) appears in a pharmacy locator and nothing more.
8. Register with a primary care practice before you need one. In a town with few practices, the wait to become a new patient is the constraint, not the wait for an appointment.

## The hospital, and the address we refuse to print

Saint Luke's Health System describes [Wright Memorial Hospital](/place/wright-memorial-hospital/) as a 25-bed acute care hospital in Trenton with a 24-hour emergency department, inpatient and outpatient diagnostic testing, surgery, rehabilitation, a specialty clinic and adjoining primary care offices.

Our sources give **two different street addresses** for it, with a third appearing in aggregator data. We publish none of them. A directory that prints one of two conflicting addresses for a hospital is guessing with somebody else's emergency, and the cost of being wrong is not symmetric with the benefit of being right. Use the operator's own page, which is linked from the listing, or a mapping service you trust.

The related caution: the [Physicians' Group](/place/wright-memorial-physicians-group/) address in our data sits one number away from one of the disputed hospital addresses. Treat both as one open question rather than two independent facts, and confirm which building you are going to when you book.

## Primary care, and the thing to do early

Saint Luke's describes the Physicians' Group as the primary care practice affiliated with the hospital, providing family medicine from paediatrics through geriatrics.

The practical advice needs no confirmed fact: **establish care before you need it.** In a town with a small number of practices, the binding constraint is usually whether a practice is accepting new patients at all, and that is a question with a waiting list attached rather than a yes or no. Ask it in your first week here, not in the week you are ill. If you take a regular medication, ask at the same time how prescriptions transfer from an out-of-area prescriber, because that is the second thing that goes wrong for new arrivals.

## Specialists, and what "specialty clinic" actually means

Saint Luke's describes a specialty clinic operated in connection with the hospital, hosting visiting specialists for Trenton-area patients.

A visiting-specialist model is common in rural hospitals and it works differently from a resident department. One source listed a roster of specialties; we could not corroborate it and we do not publish one, because a specialty listed here and not present in reality is a wasted day for someone who is unwell. Four questions get you the real position:

1. Which specialties visit this clinic, and on what cycle?
2. Do I need a referral from a local primary care provider first?
3. If my specialty is not on the visiting list, where does the health system usually refer patients — and does the referral or the patient arrange it?
4. Can follow-ups be done by video, or does each one require the drive?

Question four is the one that changes a year of appointments into a manageable number of days off work.

## Public health: the office people forget

The [Grundy County Health Department](/place/grundy-county-health-department/) is one of the strongest records in our whole dataset — the state health agency's registry and the department's own site describe it independently and agree. Between them they describe it issuing vital records including birth certificates, running immunisation and community clinics, administering WIC, and publishing local food inspection records.

For a new resident, that is three separate errands in one building: the birth certificate a school enrolment asks for, the immunisation record it asks for next, and — if you are choosing where to eat — the inspection records the department publishes.

## Pharmacy, dental and behavioural health

**Pharmacy.** [Hy-Vee Pharmacy](/place/hy-vee-pharmacy/) is described by the chain as offering delivery, durable medical equipment, immunisations, a drive-up window and compounding. Note that pharmacy counter hours are usually not store hours; we hold neither. [Evercare Pharmacy](/place/evercare-pharmacy/) appears in a pharmacy locator directory under a Trenton, Missouri location and nothing further — we hold a name, and we are showing you that we hold only a name.

**Dermatology.** [Cleaver Dermatology](/place/cleaver-dermatology/) appears in a May 2025 Chamber of Commerce announcement of a Trenton office opening. We hold no contact details for it.

**Dental and behavioural health.** We hold no sourced dental practice and no sourced behavioural health provider in Trenton. That is a gap in our research, and we are not going to disguise it as an absence in the town. If you know of either, [tell us](/submit/) — this is the most useful correction anyone could send us on this page.

**Support services.** [Green Hills Community Action Agency](/place/green-hills-community-action-agency/) runs family support programmes from a base in Trenton, and the [Grundy County Senior Center](/place/grundy-county-senior-center/) provides meals and services for older adults, as both are described by our sources. We hold no programme lists for either.

## What you will drive for, and how to plan it

We cannot tell you which services are unavailable locally, because that would require a service list we do not hold. What our data does show is the travel pattern: OATS Transit's Grundy County page describes runs toward Kansas City, St. Joseph and Chillicothe, which is a fair map of where regional appointments land. If nobody in the household drives, the transport booking *is* the appointment — confirm how far ahead a ride must be reserved before you accept a slot.

## What we could not confirm

Every hour, phone number, address, specialty, price and insurance arrangement on this page. We have opened none of the pages we cite; they reached us through search results, and nobody here has spoken to any provider. The hospital address is a live dispute, the specialty roster is unresolved, and dental and behavioural health are outright gaps. In an emergency, call 911.
