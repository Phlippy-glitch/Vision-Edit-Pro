## Who this is for

You have already decided to move, and you want the tasks in the order they actually happen. That is what this page is. It is not a page about whether to move here — that argument is on our [honest look at living in Trenton](/guides/is-trenton-a-good-place-to-live/).

This page prints no fee, no deposit, no office hour and no deadline. Every one of those numbers is held by the office that sets it, and it changes without telling us. What we can give you is the sequence, the one distinction that governs half of these errands, and the questions worth asking while you have someone on the phone.

## The short version

1. Find out whether your address is inside Trenton city limits or in Grundy County outside them. Several steps below branch on that answer.
2. Start utility service in your name before move-in day, and ask about a deposit while you are on the call.
3. Ask what trash and recycling will add to that same utility statement.
4. Change your address with the post office and with anyone who mails you anything with a deadline.
5. Declare your personal property with the [Grundy County Assessor](/place/grundy-county-assessor/), then get whatever tax document the [Collector-Treasurer](/place/grundy-county-collector-treasurer/) issues for a new arrival.
6. Only then deal with vehicle licensing and your driver licence, using the state's own office locator.
7. Register to vote with the [Grundy County Clerk](/place/grundy-county-clerk/).
8. Enrol children with [Trenton R-IX](/place/trenton-r-ix-school-district/), and collect immunisation records and birth certificates from the [Grundy County Health Department](/place/grundy-county-health-department/) first.
9. Get a library card, and establish primary care before you need it.

## Are you inside city limits? Answer that first

Inside Trenton city limits, the City of Trenton's own billing pages describe [Trenton Municipal Utilities](/place/trenton-municipal-utilities/) as the city-owned provider of electric, water and wastewater service. Outside them, [Grundy Electric Cooperative](/place/grundy-electric-cooperative/) describes itself as a member-owned cooperative serving rural members across northern Missouri and southern Iowa. A rural address and a town address are therefore two different phone calls, two different account setups and two different billing cycles.

Do not assume from a mailing address. A Trenton, MO 64683 postal address is not the same thing as an address inside Trenton city limits, and the two boundaries do not have to agree. Ask the utility to check the exact address, and ask before you sign a lease if you can.

## Trash and recycling arrive on the utility bill

This is the part that surprises people. The City of Trenton's pages describe residential trash collection and recycling as city-contracted services, currently reported as [Rapid Removal Disposal](/place/rapid-removal-disposal/) for trash and [Hope Haven Industries](/place/hope-haven-industries/) for recycling, with residents billed through the utility rather than by the hauler.

Two consequences follow, and both land on new residents. The office that bills you is not the company that drives the truck, so a missed pickup and a wrong charge are two different phone calls. And those contracts can be re-bid, which means the hauler's name on this page has a shelf life. Ask who holds each contract now before you argue about a line on your statement.

The City's own material also states that the recycling fee is billed to every utility account with water service, used or not. If that matters to your budget, ask about it on the first call rather than after the first bill.

## Property, then taxes, then the vehicle

Missouri runs this in an order that catches new arrivals who try it backwards.

The [Assessor](/place/grundy-county-assessor/) maintains the list of taxable real and personal property in Grundy County — this is where you declare what you own. The [Collector-Treasurer](/place/grundy-county-collector-treasurer/) collects those taxes, and the county's own page states that this office issues the paid-tax receipt that vehicle licensing requires.

If you arrived after the assessment date and were never assessed here, you will not have a receipt to hand over. Ask the assessor's office what it issues to a new resident instead. That is the first question of the errand, not the last one, and asking it in the wrong order is what turns one trip into three.

## Licensing: the one to be careful about

We publish no address for a Trenton license office, deliberately. Missouri's Department of Revenue announced that the office serving Trenton closed after May 31, 2023, with a transition to a new contractor. Aggregator and "DMV" sites still list a local office, and one of the numbers they repeat is identical to the Chamber of Commerce's, which is the signature of copied data rather than a working line.

Use the Department of Revenue's own office locator, and confirm the location the day before you drive.

## The rest of the list

**Mail.** The [Trenton Post Office](/place/trenton-post-office/) is the retail counter for ZIP 64683. Its listing states it does not offer passport services, which is worth knowing before you plan around it.

**Voting.** The [County Clerk](/place/grundy-county-clerk/) handles voter registration and polling places.

**Schools.** Enrolment, grade bands and what to bring are covered in [schools in Trenton](/guides/schools/). Collect birth certificates and immunisation records first; the [Health Department](/place/grundy-county-health-department/) describes issuing vital records and running immunisation clinics.

**Library.** [Grundy County Jewett Norris Library](/place/grundy-county-jewett-norris-library/) serves the town and the county.

**No car?** OATS Transit publishes a Grundy County page describing demand-response service with advance reservations required. Our copy of its schedule and fares is several years old, so treat it as a lead and call the operator.

## What we could not confirm

All of it, in the sense that matters: no office hour, deposit, fee, tax rate, contract date or service boundary on this page has been checked with anyone. We have not opened the pages we cite; they reached us through search results. The licence-office situation is the single largest open question here, and the utility service boundary is the second. If you complete any of these errands, [tell us what you found](/submit/) — that is how this page gets sharper.
