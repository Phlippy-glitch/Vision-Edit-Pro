## Who this is for

Two readers, and the page serves both. If you are considering the college, this is what the town around it does and does not offer. If you live in Trenton and have never set foot on campus, this is the part of the college that is a town amenity rather than a student one.

It is not an admissions page and it is not a course catalog. We hold no program lists, no tuition, no deadlines and no enrollment figure, and we would rather point you at the college's own pages than paraphrase them into something subtly wrong.

## The short version

1. [North Central Missouri College](/place/north-central-missouri-college/) is a public community college headquartered in downtown Trenton.
2. Its own pages describe transfer programs, career programs, adult education and student support services.
3. It operates the Trenton campus, the [Barton Farm Campus](/place/ncmc-barton-farm-campus/) outside town, and a campus in Savannah.
4. Teams are the Pirates. The baseball home field is at [Eastside Park](/place/eastside-park/), a city park.
5. The [Ketcham Community Center](/place/ncmc-ketcham-community-center/) is a college facility with community use and available memberships — confirm its current state before you count on it.
6. A student without a car should plan around a downtown campus, one supermarket and a public library within the town.
7. For anything about cost, admission or programs, use the college's own site. We publish none of it.

## What the college is, in its own description

The college describes itself as a public community college headquartered in downtown Trenton, offering transfer programs, career programs, adult education and student support services. Its athletics teams are the Pirates.

Two things follow for a resident. A community college headquartered in a town of a few thousand is a significant local employer as well as an education provider. And it means the town holds a route to a two-year credential, a career program or adult education without anybody having to move — which is not the norm for towns this size and is one of the more consequential facts on this whole site.

## The campuses, and why the distinction matters

**Trenton (main).** Downtown, on Main Street per the college's own campus-locations page.

**[Barton Farm Campus](/place/ncmc-barton-farm-campus/).** The college's agriculture campus, described as about a mile from Trenton off Highway 65, with classroom and training buildings, grazing paddocks, crop test plots and a pond. It sits outside town, in Grundy County — our sources disagreed on the compass direction, so we do not state one. If you are heading there, get directions from the college rather than from a search result that may have resolved to the main campus.

**Savannah.** A further campus, in a different part of the state. Worth knowing because a search for "NCMC" will return material about all three, and a page describing "the campus" may not mean the one in Trenton.

## The Ketcham Community Center as a town facility

The college describes the [Ketcham Community Center](/place/ncmc-ketcham-community-center/) as its athletic and community facility in Trenton, used for college sports, physical education classes, commencement and community activities, and states that memberships are available.

For a town with a summer-only municipal pool and a set of outdoor parks, an indoor facility with community memberships is a meaningful year-round option. One caveat, and it is a real one: a July 2025 college athletics release reported the building undergoing renovation work. That report is over a year old as of this page's date, which is exactly the age at which a status claim becomes unreliable in both directions — we can neither say the work continues nor say it has finished. **Call before you go, and ask specifically what is open rather than whether the building is.**

Questions worth asking on that call: what a community membership includes and costs, which hours are reserved for college use, whether the facility is available during commencement and home-game weeks, and whether any part is closed for work.

## Where the college and the town overlap

- **[Eastside Park](/place/eastside-park/)** holds the college baseball team's home field alongside the high school's football and baseball fields, per the town's tourism site. School, college and city sport share ground here.
- **[Riverside Country Club](/place/riverside-country-club/)** is described by college athletics as a facility it uses. Whether the course is open to public play or is members-only was not established in our research, so do not assume you can turn up and play.
- **The [public library](/place/grundy-county-jewett-norris-library/)** serves the town and the county, and is a short walk from the downtown campus.

## What a student needs in town

Practical shape, not endorsements. Groceries: [Hy-Vee](/place/hy-vee/) is the full-service supermarket in our records, with an in-store pharmacy; [Dollar General](/place/dollar-general/) and [Casey's](/place/caseys/) cover the smaller runs. Eating out: a short list of independent kitchens, most of them downtown, none with hours we can confirm. Health: a hospital with a 24-hour emergency department as described by the health system that operates it, plus a county health department — see [health care in Trenton](/guides/health-care/). Banking, a post office and a library are all in town.

Housing: we hold no student housing information at all, on or off campus. Ask the college directly; this is a gap in our data, not a gap in the town.

Transport: a downtown campus is walkable from downtown addresses. Anything beyond that assumes a car, and the alternative is demand-response transit with advance booking rather than a scheduled service.

## Athletics and public events

The Pirates play, the community center hosts commencement, and the fairgrounds and the school performing arts venue carry other public events in the town. Schedules for all of these live with their organizers, and we publish none of them, because a wrong date on a game or a ceremony sends someone out for nothing. The college's athletics site carries its own calendar.

## What we could not confirm

Enrollment, programs, tuition, term dates, membership terms, facility hours, the current renovation status of the community center, and the direction of the farm campus from town.
