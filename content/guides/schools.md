## Who this is for

You have a child to enrol, or you are weighing a move and want to know how schooling here is structured. This page covers the public district and the grade bands, the order to do enrollment in, and the two numbers we will not print.

It is not a page ranking schools or interpreting test scores. We hold no performance data for Trenton R-IX, and a directory that grades a real school district from search snippets is doing something worse than nothing. The state education department publishes the report cards; read them at the source, with their years attached.

## The name, before anything else

State records give the district's legal name as **Trenton R-IX**, with the roman numeral. The district's own web address and everyday local usage render it **R-9** — including the name of the [performing arts center](/place/trenton-r9-performing-arts-center/) on the school campus.

This is not trivia. It is the reason a search, a state database lookup or an online form can come back empty for a district that plainly exists. If one spelling fails, try the other before you conclude the record is missing.

## The short version

1. The district is [Trenton R-IX](/place/trenton-r-ix-school-district/), covering PK-12 across three schools.
2. [S.M. Rissler Elementary](/place/sm-rissler-elementary-school/) takes PK-4, including the district preschool program.
3. [Trenton Middle School](/place/trenton-middle-school/) takes grades 5-8.
4. [Trenton High School](/place/trenton-high-school/) takes grades 9-12. Teams are the Bulldogs, and the school is a member of the state activities association.
5. Before you book an enrollment appointment, gather birth certificate, immunisation records, proof of your address and the previous school's details.
6. If you are new to the county, contact the [Health Department](/place/grundy-county-health-department/) about vital records and immunisations first — that step has a lead time.
7. Ask the district office which school your address feeds and what its registration process is this year. The district's own site is the authority on both.
8. For anything after grade 12 without relocating, [North Central Missouri College](/place/north-central-missouri-college/) is in town.

## How the grade bands split

Three schools, three bands, no overlap in what our sources describe: elementary through grade 4, middle school for 5 through 8, high school for 9 through 12. For a family arriving with children in more than one band, that means up to three sets of start times, calendars and contacts — worth confirming as one question on the first call rather than discovering in week one.

The high school's athletic home fields are at [Eastside Park](/place/eastside-park/) per the town's tourism site, which also lists the college's baseball home field there. In a town this size, school facilities and community facilities are frequently the same buildings, and the [performing arts center](/place/trenton-r9-performing-arts-center/) on the campus is described as a venue for both school and community events.

## Enrolling a new student: the order that saves a second trip

Nothing in this section is a district rule we have confirmed; it is the shape the task takes, and the order that keeps documents from becoming the bottleneck.

1. **Gather documents before you contact anyone.** Expect to be asked for a birth certificate, immunisation records, proof of the address you are enrolling from, and records or a transcript from the previous school. Custody or guardianship paperwork matters where it applies.
2. **Deal with the health documents first.** They are the ones with a lead time, because they come from another organization. The [Grundy County Health Department](/place/grundy-county-health-department/) describes issuing vital records including birth certificates, and running immunisation clinics.
3. **Ask the previous school to send records directly.** Districts generally prefer a school-to-school transfer over a parent-carried copy, and starting that early removes a common delay.
4. **Then contact the district.** Ask which school the address feeds, what the current registration route is (in person, online or both), what the appointment needs, and what the deadline is for a start date you are aiming at.
5. **Ask about the things that are not on the enrollment form**: transport eligibility, meal programs and their application, and special education or 504 processes if any apply. These have their own timelines and their own staff.

## The questions worth asking on the call

- Which school does this exact address feed, and is that settled or subject to a boundary?
- What is the current registration process, and is any part of it online?
- Which documents must be originals rather than copies?
- What is the school calendar for the year I am arriving in, and where is it published?
- How is transport determined, and by what distance rule?
- Who is the contact for special education services, and what do you need from a previous district?
- What does the school need from a family that has moved mid-year?

## Two numbers we are not printing

**Enrollment.** Our sources gave two different district enrollment counts. Publishing either would mean picking a winner between publishers we cannot adjudicate between, so we publish neither. The state education department reports district enrollment with a year attached; that is the version worth quoting.

**A phone number flagged in research.** One contact number that surfaced for a school in our research did not survive scrutiny and does not appear anywhere on this site. Use the numbers on the district's own pages, and treat any school contact detail you find on an aggregator as a lead rather than a fact.

## After grade 12, without moving

[North Central Missouri College](/place/north-central-missouri-college/) is a public community college headquartered in downtown Trenton, with an agriculture campus outside town and a campus in Savannah. Its own pages describe transfer programs, career programs, adult education and student support services. For a family with a teenager, a two-year college in town is a genuine structural difference from a comparable town without one. Our [college guide](/guides/ncmc/) covers what it means for residents as well as students.
