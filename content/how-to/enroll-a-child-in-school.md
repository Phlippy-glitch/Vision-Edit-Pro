## Start with the district office

**Enrollment for a Trenton address runs through the [Trenton R-IX School District](/place/trenton-r-ix-school-district/), and the district office is the authority on which school your address feeds and what this year's process is.** Everything below is preparation, so that one appointment does the job.

We print no enrollment deadline, office hour or fee. None has been checked with the district, and a school calendar moves without telling a directory.

## The name, before you fill in anything

State records give the legal name as **Trenton R-IX**, using the roman numeral. The district's own web address and everyday local use render it **R-9**.

This is not trivia. It is why a state database lookup, a records-transfer form or an online search can come back empty for a district that plainly exists. If one spelling fails, try the other before you conclude the record is missing.

## Who takes which grades

Per the district's own material: [S.M. Rissler Elementary](/place/sm-rissler-elementary-school/) takes PK-4 including the district preschool program, [Trenton Middle School](/place/trenton-middle-school/) takes grades 5-8, and [Trenton High School](/place/trenton-high-school/) takes grades 9-12.

For a family arriving with children in more than one band, that means up to three sets of start times, calendars and drop-off routines. Ask about all three on one call rather than discovering the differences in week one.

## Gather these before you go

- **Birth certificate** or the proof of age the district accepts.
- **Immunisation records.** This is the item with a lead time. The [Grundy County Health Department](/place/grundy-county-health-department/) describes issuing vital records and running immunisation clinics, and if a shot is missing you want to know weeks ahead, not the morning of.
- **Proof of residence** at the address you are enrolling from.
- **The previous school's name and contact,** plus a recent report card or transcript if you have one.
- **Any special education paperwork** — an existing plan, evaluation reports, health care plans, allergy or medication documentation.
- **Custody or guardianship documents,** where they apply. Schools have to work from what is on file.

## The order to do it in

1. Call the district office. Confirm which school, what the enrollment process is this year, and what to bring.
2. Request records from the previous school in the same week. Records transfer takes as long as it takes, and starting it early is free.
3. Book the health department for anything missing from the immunisation record.
4. Attend the enrollment appointment with the folder complete.
5. Ask about transport, meals and start times before you leave.
6. For a high school student, ask about activities eligibility separately — transfer rules for athletics are set by the state association, not by the school.

## What goes wrong

**Immunisation gaps found late.** The commonest cause of a delayed start, and entirely within your control if you check early.

**Assuming records follow automatically.** They usually need a request, and a mid-year move is when this hurts most.

**Special education services starting from scratch.** Bring the existing paperwork rather than describing it. Services are built on documents.

**Mid-year arrivals losing a week.** Call the district before the move rather than after it. Nothing about the process requires you to live here yet to ask a question.

**Activities eligibility.** A high schooler who has changed schools may face conditions on competing. Ask early, because the answer can change how you plan a move date.

## What to ask when you call

1. Which school does my address feed, and has that assignment changed recently?
2. What exactly do I bring, and is there anything I can submit ahead of the appointment?
3. What is the process and timeline for transferring records from the previous school?
4. What are start and dismissal times at each school my children will attend?
5. Does my address have bus service, and how do I sign up for meals?
6. For a high school transfer, what does activities eligibility depend on, and who decides it?

## If something goes wrong

Take it back to the district office first — assignment, records, transport and eligibility questions all live there. For services a student is entitled to, the state education department publishes the compliance and special education material and is the authority above the district. For a health record you cannot obtain, the health department is the office that issues it. For how schooling here is structured, see our guide to [schools in Trenton](/guides/schools/).
