## The short answer, and the trap

**The newspaper most search results still point you at is not publishing.** The Trenton Republican-Times, which had served Trenton and Grundy County since 1864, closed after its June 19, 2025 issues. Its former domain still resolves and still ranks well, and nothing about a newspaper-shaped page announces that the newspaper has stopped.

The outlets to use instead: **[KTTN / KGOZ radio](/place/kttn-kgoz-radio/)** for day-to-day coverage of the town and county, and **[The Trenton Telegraph](/place/trenton-telegraph/)**, a weekly launched in September 2025, for the fuller written account.

## The outlets that are live

**Radio.** KTTN describes itself as a three-station group based in Trenton covering north Missouri — KTTN-FM 92.3, KTTN-AM 1600 and KGOZ-FM 101.7 — with local newscasts airing six times a day and sportscasts three times a day, per the station's own contact page. In our research this was the outlet doing the routine local reporting: fair dates, board decisions, the newspaper transition itself. If you follow one source, follow this one.

**The weekly.** [The Trenton Telegraph](/place/trenton-telegraph/) launched in September 2025, owned by the Grundy County Industrial Development Corporation with CherryRoad Media providing management. Its own description covers schools and youth sports, town government, community events and business news. Note what a weekly means for timing: by the time news reaches you, it can be up to seven days old.

**The bodies themselves.** For meetings, notices, closures and elections, the organization publishes before anyone reports it — the [City of Trenton](/place/city-of-trenton/) for municipal matters, the [Grundy County Clerk](/place/grundy-county-clerk/) for elections and polling places, [Trenton R-IX](/place/trenton-r-ix-school-district/) for school calendars and board business.

**Event organizers.** [Main Street Trenton](/place/main-street-trenton/) for downtown events and the market, and the Chamber for the town's festival. Organisers change dates; calendars that copy organizers do not always follow.

## The one to be careful with

We list the [Trenton Republican-Times](/place/trenton-republican-times/) because a county newspaper of that length of run is part of the record. We list it as closed, and **its website field on this site is deliberately empty**, so no template can render it as a live link no matter how the record is reused later.

Why this matters more than a normal stale listing: a closed shop sends someone on a wasted drive, which is bad. A dead newspaper presented as a live news source sends someone looking for information that will never arrive, about a place they are trying to understand — and they will not realise it for weeks.

## How to check something time-critical

1. Go to the responsible body first. A meeting notice, a closure or a deadline originates there.
2. Check the radio group for anything already decided rather than merely scheduled.
3. Check the weekly for the fuller account, allowing for its publication cycle.
4. If two sources disagree on a date or a time, treat the organizer as correct and the reprint as stale.
5. If it is urgent — a road closure, an outage, an emergency — call the responsible office rather than reading anything, this site included. For an emergency, call 911.

## What to ask, if you are calling around

1. Where do you publish your meeting agendas and notices, and how far ahead?
2. Is there a mailing list or an alert I can sign up for?
3. Who do I contact to submit an event, an obituary or a correction?
4. Is there an archive of the closed newspaper, and where is it held?

Question four is worth asking at the [library](/place/grundy-county-jewett-norris-library/). Newspaper archives are a common county-library holding, and a run beginning in 1864 is the kind of record that outlives the publication.

## What we could not confirm

Whether any outlet named here is publishing or broadcasting on the day you read this; current ownership or management of the weekly; the frequencies, schedules and newscast counts described on the radio group's own page. Our record of the launch rests on 2025 reporting, and 2025 reporting ages. We have not opened the pages cited below and have contacted no publication; we last looked at these sources on 15 August 2026.
