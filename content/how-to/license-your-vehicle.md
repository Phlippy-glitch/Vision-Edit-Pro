## Do not drive to a Trenton license office

**The Missouri Department of Revenue announced that the license office serving Trenton closed after May 31, 2023, with a transition to a new contractor.** Aggregator and DMV-style sites still list a local office, and one of the addresses they repeat also appears in our sources as a Chamber of Commerce address — the signature of copied data, not a working counter.

**We could not confirm where the closest open office is today.** That is exactly the kind of thing to settle by phone before you burn a morning. Use the Department of Revenue's own office locator, linked below, then call the office you find to confirm it is open and takes walk-ins on the day you plan to go — whatever any listing anywhere says.

## Do the county paperwork first

Missouri runs this errand in an order that catches people who attempt it backwards.

1. **[Grundy County Assessor](/place/grundy-county-assessor/)** — where taxable personal property is declared. If you were never assessed here, there is nothing for the collector to issue you.
2. **[Grundy County Collector-Treasurer](/place/grundy-county-collector-treasurer/)** — the county's own page describes this office as issuing the paid-tax receipt that vehicle licensing requires. Both offices are in the [courthouse](/place/grundy-county-courthouse/).
3. **Then the license office**, wherever the locator sends you.

If you moved here recently and were never assessed, you will not have a receipt to hand over. **Ask the assessor's office what it issues to a new resident instead.** That is question one of the errand, not question three, and asking it in the wrong order is what turns one trip into three.

## What to have in the folder

Expect some combination of the following, and confirm the list with the office you are actually going to — requirements differ by transaction:

- The title or ownership paperwork, signed correctly by the seller. An incorrectly signed title is the commonest reason a transaction stops at the counter.
- The county tax receipt or statement of non-assessment described above.
- Current proof of insurance for the vehicle.
- A safety inspection and, where required, an emissions certificate. Ask whether your vehicle needs one before you pay for one.
- Your identification, and any lien paperwork.
- A payment method the office accepts. Contractor-run counters do not all take the same cards, and that is a bad thing to discover at the front of the queue.

## The deadlines and the traps

**Titling has a clock on it.** Missouri charges a penalty for titling a vehicle late after a purchase, and it grows the longer it runs. We do not print the window or the amount, because we have not checked either. Ask what your deadline is on your first call.

**Fees vary by transaction and by weight, and some offices add a processing charge.** Ask what the total will be rather than budgeting from a figure you read anywhere, including here.

**Personal property tax is annual, and it bites next year.** A missed assessment cycle produces a missing receipt, and a missing receipt stops a renewal months later.

**A driver licence and a vehicle licence are different transactions,** sometimes handled at the same counter and sometimes not. Ask which the office you are going to actually does.

**Treat every third-party "DMV" listing as suspect.** They are the reason this page exists. The state agency's own locator is the authority.

## What to ask when you call

1. Is this office open on the day I want to come, and does it take walk-ins or appointments?
2. Which transactions do you handle — titling, plates, renewals, licences?
3. Exactly which documents do you need for my transaction, and what makes a title invalid at your counter?
4. What is my titling deadline given the date on my bill of sale, and what is the penalty if I miss it?
5. What forms of payment do you take, and what will the total be for this transaction?

## Who to call if it goes wrong

A rejected document, a title problem or a dispute over what is required is a Department of Revenue matter — start with the state agency, not the contractor at the counter. A missing tax receipt is a county matter and goes back to the assessor and the collector. If a listing anywhere sent you to a closed office, tell whoever published it; if it was us, [tell us](/submit/).
