## Three levels of government, in this order

**State registration first, then the county, then the city — and the city question before you sign a lease.** That order avoids the two expensive kinds of re-work: registering an entity you then rename, and committing to premises you cannot legally use.

We print no fee and no processing time. None has been checked with any office, and the numbers that matter here change with the code and the tax year.

## The sequence

1. **Register the business with the state.** Entity formation, a registered agent and your federal employer identification number come first. Local offices ask for them.
2. **Register for state taxes** — sales tax and employer withholding if they apply to you. A sales tax number is often a prerequisite for a county merchant's license.
3. **Call the [City of Trenton](/place/city-of-trenton/) before you sign anything.** Ask whether the activity is permitted at that address, what license it needs and whether the space needs an occupancy inspection. Zoning is the question that kills a lease.
4. **Ask the county what it needs.** A merchant's license, where it applies, is a county matter — ask the [Collector-Treasurer](/place/grundy-county-collector-treasurer/) — and business equipment is declared to the [Assessor](/place/grundy-county-assessor/).
5. **Handle the premises.** Fit-out work usually means permits and inspections; see [getting a building permit](/how-to/get-a-building-permit/).
6. **Open utility accounts in the business name** with [Trenton Municipal Utilities](/place/trenton-municipal-utilities/) if you are inside city limits, and ask about a commercial deposit while you are on the call.
7. **Then market yourself.** [Main Street Trenton](/place/main-street-trenton/) and the [Chamber](/place/trenton-area-chamber-of-commerce/) come after you can legally open, not before.

## Sector-specific approvals that catch people

**Food and drink** brings a health inspection into the sequence, with a lead time. Ask what it covers and how far ahead to book, because an opening date set before the inspection is booked is a date you will move.

**Anything with a sign** typically needs its own permit, and downtown properties may carry extra constraints. Ask before the sign is fabricated.

**Home-based businesses are not exempt by default.** Ask the city what a home occupation may do at a residential address — customer traffic, signage, deliveries and storage are the usual limits.

**Alcohol, childcare, contracting, personal care and vehicle trades** are commonly state-licensed on top of anything local. Assume an extra layer and ask which regulator holds it.

## Where the local help is

The [Trenton Area Chamber of Commerce](/place/trenton-area-chamber-of-commerce/) describes itself as maintaining a member directory and organizing the town's festival. [Main Street Trenton](/place/main-street-trenton/) describes a downtown directory, downtown events, a Saturday farmers market and a facade grant program — that grant is worth asking about before you spend on a storefront, not after.

For anything larger — a site, a building, workforce or incentive questions — the [North Central Missouri Development Alliance](/place/north-central-missouri-development-alliance/) and the [Green Hills Regional Planning Commission](/place/green-hills-regional-planning-commission/) are the regional bodies. Planning commissions administer grant programs that individual small towns cannot, which makes them a useful early call for a project with a building attached.

One caution: the development alliance's Grundy County profile in our sources is dated 2018. Treat any figure from it as historical unless you find it restated with a recent date.

## What to ask, and who to ask

**Ask the city:** Is this activity allowed at this address? What license do I need, what does it cost, and how long does it take? Is an occupancy inspection required before I open?

**Ask the county:** Does a merchant's license apply to what I sell, and what do you need to issue one? How and when do I declare business personal property, and what happens if I open mid-year?

**Ask the utility:** What deposit does a commercial account require, and what is the lead time for service in the business name?

**Ask the Chamber and Main Street:** What does membership actually include, and what does it cost? Which of your programs is open to a business at my stage?

## If it goes wrong

An inspection failure or a license refusal goes back to the office that issued it, in person, with the paperwork. What the rules say can be checked against the municipal code, published online and linked below. If one office contradicts another, get both answers in writing — that is the fastest route to a resolution and the only record you will have later.
