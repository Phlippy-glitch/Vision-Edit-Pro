This is a directory of Trenton, Missouri, assembled from public sources and published with the source shown next to the facts it supports. This page explains exactly how it was built, because the method is the only thing that should persuade you to trust any of it.

## What we did

We searched for every business, office and public place we could find evidence of in Trenton and Grundy County. For each one we recorded what was claimed, which publisher claimed it, and which specific fields that publisher supports. Where two unrelated publishers agreed on the same detail, we recorded that too, and you will see it noted on the page.

We checked every record against Missouri, Grundy County, the local ZIP code and the local area code. There are Trentons in New Jersey, Michigan and Georgia, and a directory that quietly mixes them is the most common way this kind of site goes wrong.

Where sources disagreed, we did not choose a winner. We publish the disagreement and leave the field empty.

## What we did not do

**We did not open the pages we cite.** Our network could not reach them. Everything here came to us through search results that quote those pages second-hand — real URLs and real publishers, but a summary of a page rather than the page.

We did not call anyone. We did not visit. Nobody working on this site has been to Trenton.

That means **no fact on this site has been confirmed by us**, including facts that come from an organisation's own website. It also means we do not describe how anywhere looks, sounds or feels, and we never will unless we are describing a photograph we hold. We hold none.

## What we refuse to publish

- **Guesses.** A field we could not source is shown as "not confirmed", never filled in and never silently dropped.
- **Reviews, ratings and star counts.** We cannot verify them and they invite manipulation.
- **Paid placement.** Nothing here can be bought, and listings are ordered alphabetically.
- **Emergency phone numbers.** We publish no phone number for police, fire or any emergency service, from an unconfirmed source, ever. A wrong number there is not a defect, it is a harm. In an emergency, call 911.
- **One address for a place where our sources give two.** You will see us say so on the page.
- **The names of people holding public office.** Offices age well; naming the wrong person does not, and there is no reader benefit that justifies it.
- **Business descriptions we wrote ourselves.** If a listing does not say what a business does, it is because no source told us.

## What you should do

**Call ahead.** Before you drive anywhere on the strength of this site, ring the place or check the operator's own page — both are linked on every listing. Treat our hours, addresses and phone numbers as a starting point for that call, not as a substitute for it.

This matters most for the things that change quietly: hours, seasonal closures, and anything a business decided last week.

## What would change this page

The notice at the top of every page comes down for a record when a person opens that record's sources directly, or calls, and logs it. Not before, and not all at once — it will lift record by record. Until then the notice stays, and it stays on every page, because a claim made once quietly and then dropped is not a disclosure.

## Corrections

Every page and every listing has a correction link, and the fastest route to a correct listing is the person who owns the business. We publish corrections with the date they were made, on [our corrections page](/corrections/). If we get something wrong about you, tell us and we will fix it, or remove the listing entirely if that is what you would rather.

## Who we are not

We are not the City of Trenton, Grundy County, the Chamber of Commerce, or any organisation listed here, and nothing on this site is endorsed by them. Where we link to an official page, it is the official page that is authoritative — not us.
