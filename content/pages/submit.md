It is free. There is no paid version and no paid placement — there is nothing here to buy. A person reads every submission before it goes live, and all we need is your business name and category. Everything else is optional.

## What we publish

Your business name, your category, and whichever of address, phone, website and hours you choose to give us. **Leave anything blank and we leave it blank.** We would much rather show nothing than show a wrong phone number, and a blank field on our page says "not confirmed" rather than guessing on your behalf.

If you tell us the correct version of something we got wrong, we will say on the page that it was corrected, and when. We will not say who told us.

## What we don't do

We do not sell leads. We do not run ads on listing pages. We do not host reviews, ratings or stars, so nothing you send us can be buried by them. We are not the Chamber of Commerce and we are not the City — we list members and non-members alike, and if you have a Chamber page we will link to it.

We will not publish a home address or a personal mobile number, even if we found one. Tell us which contact you want public and that is the only one that appears.

## How to reach us right now

Being straight with you about the mechanism, since this page would otherwise promise more than it can do: **this site is a set of static pages with no form handler behind it.** There is no submission form yet, no account system, and no automated email. Anyone who tells you otherwise on a site like this is describing software that does not exist.

What does work today:

- **Open an issue** on the repository that builds this site. It is public, it is dated, and it is the fastest route: [file a correction](https://github.com/Phlippy-glitch/Vision-Edit-Pro/issues/new).
- Include the listing name, what is wrong, and what it should say. If you are the owner, say so — an owner's correction outranks every source we have.

When a form exists, this section will describe it and nothing else will change.

## Why we are asking you

We built this site from sources we could name but could not open, and nothing on it has been confirmed. You are the person who actually knows whether your hours are right. A correction from you is worth more than anything we could look up, which is why this page exists and why it is free.
