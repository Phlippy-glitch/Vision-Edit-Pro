This site is published under the name **the Trenton Directory Project**. That is a masthead, not a person — this page states plainly what sits behind it, because a site that asks you to trust its sourcing owes you the same clarity about itself.

## Independent and non-commercial

We are not funded by, run by, or endorsed by the City of Trenton, Grundy County, or the Chamber of Commerce. Nothing here is for sale. No listing paid to appear, no listing can pay to rank higher, and entries are ordered alphabetically because that is the only ordering that cannot be bought.

## Not from Trenton

Nobody involved in building this lives in Trenton, and nobody involved has been there. That is a limitation, not a credential — and it is exactly why every entry names its source, why nothing on this site is presented as confirmed, and why [the corrections path](/submit/) matters more here than on most sites. The people who actually know this town are the ones reading it.

## What we do

We compile what named public sources say about the businesses, offices and places of Trenton and Grundy County. We record which publisher supports which specific fact. We publish disagreements between sources instead of picking winners. We publish corrections with the date they were made.

## What we do not do

We do not verify by phone. We do not visit. We do not accept payment. We do not host reviews or ratings. We do not rank one business over another. And we do not fill a gap with a guess — a blank field on this site means no source we found supports a value, and it stays blank until one does.
